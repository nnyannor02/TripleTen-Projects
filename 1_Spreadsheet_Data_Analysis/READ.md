# Superstore Sales Analysis

This project analyzes the Superstore dataset to evaluate key business performance metrics.  
The work focuses on identifying trends and insights across:

- Sales and profit performance  
- Regional and segment-based patterns  
- Product category and subcategory behavior  
- Customer purchasing trends  
- High-profit vs. high-loss products  
- Opportunities for business optimization  

This assignment demonstrates exploratory data analysis (EDA), metric calculation, and business interpretation using structured datasets.
