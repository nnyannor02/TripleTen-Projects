## Zomato Sales & Customer Insights Dashboard (Power BI)

**Tools:** Power BI, DAX, Data Modeling  
**Skills:** KPI creation, data cleaning, relationship modeling, trend analysis, dashboard design

This project analyzes Zomato's restaurant, order, and customer datasets to identify key revenue drivers, seasonal trends, and high-performing cuisine types. The dashboard includes custom KPIs, slicers for interactive filtering, and a clear visual breakdown of restaurant and cuisine performance.

**Key Features:**
- Yearly revenue trend analysis  
- Top restaurants and top cuisines by revenue  
- KPI cards for revenue, orders, customers, and ARPU  
- Date and cuisine slicers for dynamic filtering  
- Identification of data quality issues (missing restaurant IDs)
