# Job Search Strategy

This project outlines my personalized job search plan developed during the TripleTen Business Intelligence Analyst Program. It includes a structured approach to identifying target roles, strengthening my professional profile, and preparing for interviews.

### Key Components
- Resume optimization and professional branding  
- Identification of target roles and industries  
- Networking strategy and outreach plan  
- Skill-building roadmap (Power BI, SQL, Analytics)  
- Action steps for applications and career readiness  

This plan supports my transition into data and business intelligence roles.
